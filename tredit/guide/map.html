The map

The first tab shows the stage drawn from its own model, without textures, so the shapes are easy to read. The top view looks straight down. "Up" on the screen is the direction the start faces when its facing is 0°, and x runs to the right. The side view underneath shows heights. By default it looks along the stage's longer side; the Side view box above it lets you choose the other direction.

Scroll the mouse wheel to zoom, drag with the right mouse button to pan, and press F to fit the whole stage again. Moving the mouse over the top view shows the coordinates in the status bar, along with the height of the floor under the pointer.

Collision

Press C (or use View > Show collision) to show the surfaces the ball actually rolls on. Cyan is the stage's fixed part and violet belongs to moving parts. Collision and the drawn model are separate things in this game, so it's worth turning this on whenever you build something.

Grid and snapping

The grid can be switched off under View. When you drag something it snaps to a quarter unit; pick a different step under View > Snap when dragging, or turn snapping off to place things freely. With Keep height above the floor while dragging switched on, an object you drag keeps the same height above whatever floor is under it, so bananas stay floating at the right height as you move them across slopes and steps.

What the colours mean

The legend under the properties panel explains every symbol. A white ring marks anything you've changed or added. A dashed outline means the object belongs to a moving part and travels with it in the game.
