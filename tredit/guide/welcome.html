Welcome

This is a stage editor for Super Monkey Ball: Touch & Roll on the Nintendo DS (the USA version). You open your own copy of the game, change the stages, and save the result as a new ROM that you can play in an emulator or on hardware.

At heart it's an object placement tool. You move the start, goals, bumpers and bananas around, add new ones, and take away the ones you don't want. On top of that there are a few stage tools that come in handy: simple platforms and invisible floors, removing collision, a 3D view, the stages' moving parts and their timing, brand new empty stages, and the names shown on the world select.

It isn't 3D modelling software and doesn't try to be. If you want detailed shapes, build them in a real 3D program (Blender and Wings3D are both free) and bring them in as an .obj file. The guide page Importing 3D models explains how.

Your ROM is safe

The editor never writes to the ROM you open. Every time you save, it writes a separate copy and, next to it, a small .patch.json file that lists exactly what changed. Before a copy is written, every change is checked against the original bytes, and after it's written the copy is read back and compared with what you see in the editor. If anything doesn't match, nothing is saved and you're told why.
