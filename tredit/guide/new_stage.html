Making a new stage

Sometimes it's easier to start from nothing. Stage > Create new stage here… (also on the stage list's right-click menu) replaces the selected stage with an empty one: a single platform and the start. There's no goal and no bananas, so the first thing to do is add a goal.

Before anything happens, a window asks for the platform's length, width, height and thickness and makes sure you really want to replace the stage. The start goes near one end of the platform, facing along it. From there you build the stage like any other, with objects, platforms, invisible floors and, for new stages, imported 3D models.

The platform is drawn with one of the old stage's textures. The new stage is marked [new stage] in the list.

Changing your mind

Stage > Edit the new stage's platform… changes the starting platform's size later without touching anything you've added. Stage > Restore original stage… brings the original back. That works at any time, even after you've saved and closed the editor, because your ROM still has the original and the saved copy records that the stage was replaced. Undo works here as well.

Only the stages of the twelve worlds can be replaced.
