Objects

There are four kinds of object: the start, goals, bumpers and bananas (single bananas and bunches worth five).

Adding

Press 1 for a banana, 2 for a bunch, 3 for a bumper or 4 for a goal (or use the toolbar or the Object menu), then click on the top view where it should go. It lands on the floor under the pointer. Press Esc to change your mind. A new goal turns its front towards the start so you can roll straight into it.

Moving

Drag an object on the top view to move it across the stage, or on the side view to change its height. The arrow keys nudge the selected object by one snap step, and Page Up and Page Down raise and lower it. You can also type exact numbers into X, Y and Z on the right.

Turning

Swivel (facing) turns the start or a goal around the vertical axis. 0° points up on the top view and larger numbers turn left. Q and E turn the selected object 15° at a time; hold Shift for 1°. A goal's facing is the direction of its front, the side you roll through.

Goals and bumpers can also Tilt (lean forwards or backwards) and Rotate (roll to one side), because the game stores a full rotation for them. Bumpers swivel too. Together the three fields turn a goal or bumper any way you like, so a goal can stand on a slope or lean into a curve. The start has only a facing in the game's data, and bananas have no rotation at all because they spin by themselves.

Sizes

Goals and bumpers show a Size along their own X, Y and Z (width, height and depth, where 1 is the normal size), but you can't change it. The game would draw a bigger or smaller goal or bumper, yet it ignores the size where it matters. A bumper bounces at its normal size however big it's drawn. A bigger goal's finish line gets smaller instead of bigger and moves up, so a goal made taller than about 1.2 can't be finished at all: its finish line ends up above the ball. So the editor keeps every goal and bumper at the size stored in the game, and a copy made with Ctrl+D is always normal-sized. The start and bananas have no size, because the game stores only their position (and the start's facing).

Other settings

A goal can be made a red goal, and a banana can be turned into a bunch (worth 5) or back. The banana total at the bottom of the window counts the stage's bananas; the game can't count past 255.

Duplicate, delete and reset

Ctrl+D copies the selected object next to the original, Del deletes it, and Edit > Reset selected object to original puts an original object back exactly as it was. The start can be moved and turned but not deleted.

Objects on moving parts

Some objects belong to a part of the stage that moves, like a sliding platform. They're drawn with a dashed outline, shown where they are when the stage starts, and they ride along with their part in the game. New objects always go into the stage's fixed part.

An object has to sit inside the stage's collision area for the ball to touch it. If you put one too far out, the editor tells you instead of saving something that wouldn't work.
